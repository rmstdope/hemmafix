Postponing a chore to a later day

Where do you end up after postponing a chore?

A — Stay, focus the next choreRecommendedAgreed

You don't see the chore in its new place; the message and the tab's count tell you where it went.

Recommended: The usual pattern when an item leaves a list (as when archiving mail): you stay, and can postpone the next one at once.
